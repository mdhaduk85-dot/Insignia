Occurance Of Problem

Before even founding the team , member of insignia encountered a hard to hear individual seeing their inability to communicate effectivly , it seems a problem is identified

This moment of stalled connection wasn't just an awkward silence—it was a revelation. It laid bare the systemic communication barriers that millions face daily. By identifying this profound gap, the seeds of Insignia were sown: the realization that accessibility isn't just a feature, but a fundamental necessity for human connection.

When caught with the broad problem , we needed to identify how much impact would this create would this be sufficient ? would it be effecient ?
